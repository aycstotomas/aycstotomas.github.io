About Arnelius Yanqhul Carulla Sto. Tomas

Background

I was born in Baguio City and I am now a first-year BSIT student at the University of the Cordilleras. I serve as the Broadcasting Club Director for UC's Publication Guild and was previously a member of the University of Baguio SHS Debate Club. I chose ERP because it connects technology to how real organizations work, which fits my interest in software and strategic leadership.

Goal and Plan

My goal is to become an ERP developer who builds practical systems that help organizations run better. To get there, I will finish my BSIT degree with a focus on ERP, master Java, JavaScript, and Python along with databases, build and publish projects on GitHub, and use my leadership and public speaking to work well with teams and clients.
